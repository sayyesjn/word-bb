Claude & AI
Not connected

Type your orders in a Claude chat and Claude reads and edits the document that is open here. Keep this app open while Claude works (split-screen with Claude is handy).

Connect Claude
  1. Put the relay online (the server/ folder, see README) and type its address below.
  2. Tap Connect.
  3. In Claude: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector. Paste the connector address. Choose no sign-in.
  4. In a Claude chat say, for example: “Open my Word document and fix the spelling.”

Anyone who has the connector address can read and edit the open document. Keep it private. “New key” cuts off the old address.

Use ChatGPT, Gemini or another AI (copy and paste)
  1. Tap Copy prompt and paste it into the other AI app.
  2. Copy that AI's reply, paste it below, then tap Apply reply.
